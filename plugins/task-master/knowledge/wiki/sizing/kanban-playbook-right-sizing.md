# Do My Work Items All Need to Be the Same Size? (Kanban Playbook)

This is a page of the Kanban Playbook on prokanban.org. The raw file's `authors` and `published` fields are empty, and the text names no author.

## Grade

The source is unsigned and reports no measurement, so this wiki grades it on its mechanisms and not on its magnitudes. Every figure in it is an illustration. It builds on the [[kanban-guide]] SLE, and it comes from the same school.

## Thesis

Right-sized means "small enough to flow through your system reliably, but still deliver something a customer can use." (L16). Items that are too large hide WIP, items that are too small create a false picture of health, and both make the SLE unreliable (L18-L22). The size check is one question against the SLE (L30). Four moves find a split (L35-L49).

## Where this wiki disagrees with it

- **"Right-sizing is not estimation."** (L30). This wiki reads the page's own question, whether there is "a good chance this item will finish within our SLE", as a coarse estimate of duration with one step. What is true is that it needs no scale and no number per task. See [[size-ceiling]].
- **The SLE is unreliable until items are right-sized (L22), and items are right-sized against the SLE (L30).** The page does not notice the circle. [[size-ceiling]] gives a way out.

## What this wiki dropped, and why

- **The figures as values.** "85% of items finish in 10 days or less" (L33) belongs to the illustrated team. The 80% and 51% (L37) are arithmetic on a hypothetical. See [[compound-task-text-signs]].
- **The list of benefits** (L24-L27), such as "Items complete within your SLE more often." These assert effects with no measurement.
- **"Right-sizing is what makes your metrics trustworthy."** (L22). An unmeasured effect. The mechanism behind it, that outliers distort the history, is kept.
- **Statements about customer feeling** (L18, L20). No sign.
- **"You are not reducing scope."** (L49). It describes intent and is not testable.

## Extraction caveat

The list at L24-L27 has no lead-in, so this wiki assumes it lists benefits. The "Key Principle" label at L29 and the shift into a worked example at L33 are flattened. No quotation is affected.

## How to read it

Read it as one school's working answer for sizing without estimation. Its splitting signs are practical and sit in task text, which suits this wiki. Its central claim is more modest than it sounds, and its numbers are not evidence.
