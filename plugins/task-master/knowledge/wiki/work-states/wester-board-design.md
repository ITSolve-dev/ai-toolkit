# Designing Your Board to Focus on Flow (Wester)

This is a short post by Julia Wester, reproduced on prokanban.org "with kind permission of the author" from actionableagile.com (L63). The raw file's `authors` field is empty, and the name comes from that closing line.

## Grade

The source is vendor-adjacent. Its original home is the site of a flow-metrics product, and its links lead to that site and to a vendor's support pages (L34, L45, L51, L63). This wiki grades it on its mechanisms and not on its magnitudes, as the charter requires. It reports no measurement, and its stated basis is "at least from our experience" (L18). It also disclaims authority: "there are no board police to decide what is right and wrong." (L61).

It is also not independent of the Kanban Guide. Julia Wester is thanked among the Guide's reviewers ([[kanban-guide]] L192), so agreement between the two is one school agreeing with itself.

## Thesis

A board can cause dysfunction through the way it draws work. Five design moves are offered: name columns after the work and not the workers, plan for cycles instead of moving cards back, separate active work from waiting, keep Blocked and On Hold out of the columns, and give handoffs their own columns.

## What this wiki dropped, and why

- **Resource efficiency versus flow efficiency** (L24-L26) as a management stance. It is about who is kept busy and how a team shares ownership, which is managing people. The one observable part, a column named like a job title, is kept.
- **Team performance over individual performance** (L26). Managing people.
- **The Flow Efficiency chart** (L45). It links to a product's support page. Tool capabilities are out of scope, and the mechanism it would show is kept on [[active-versus-queued]].
- **"This results in cycle times becoming longer and longer."** (L51) as a size of effect. The mechanism, stashing work to get around a limit, is kept.
- **The closing advice** (L61) to consider your own board. It has no sign.

## How to read it

Read it as a practitioner's design tests. It says what goes wrong and how you would see it, which suits this wiki, and its claims about boards concern the work. Its mechanisms for people, such as pain being "the forcing function" (L53), need the executor note each page gives them.
