# Control questions

Written before the first source was read, so they cannot echo a page. The same questions run against
the readable base and against the compressed one, and the answers are compared.

A question here must not be copied into a page: the base is the answer key.

## In charter

1. A task reads "make the import reliable". What is missing from it, and how would you tell the
   executor is about to guess?
2. Two tasks both depend on one design decision that nobody has taken yet. Is that one task, two, or
   three, and why?
3. The team's estimates in points have drifted upward over three months. How do you tell that from the
   work getting harder?
4. A task was estimated at the size where the team's scale says to split, and the author says it cannot
   be split. What do you do?
5. Twenty bug reports came in this week. In what order do you decide whether each one is real, a
   duplicate, and how urgent it is?
6. Two features compete for the next slot and nobody can put a number on either. What do you compare?
7. A team has no iterations. What can it say about when something will be done, and what can it not say?
8. Half the tasks on the board sit in "In Review" for days. What does that tell you, and what does it
   not?
9. One board column covers both "waiting for review" and "being reviewed". What breaks?
10. Velocity fell by a third last period. What must you check before calling that a slowdown?
11. Which chart shows whether work is piling up, and what can that chart not tell you?
12. Should a two-person team fix the length of its period, and what does a change of length cost?
