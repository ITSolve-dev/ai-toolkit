This is outside what the knowledge base covers, so I won't answer it as if it were grounded. The charter lists "Scripts for meetings" and facilitation as out of scope: the plugin prepares material and does not run a ceremony. It also excludes managing people, which is where getting quiet participants to speak falls.

What the base can do for a retrospective is the data side. It covers velocity, throughput, time in state, age of open work, and comparing plan with fact and explaining the gap (see the charter, "Retrospective analysis"). If you want, ask what the board history should show before the retrospective, and I can answer that from the base.
