This is outside what the task-master knowledge base covers, so I can't answer it from the base. Its charter scopes it to organizing work for a few people steering an AI agent. It leaves out frameworks for aligning many teams, because applied at a small scale they produce ritual instead of process. Aligning twelve teams on a shared quarterly plan is that case.

What the base can help with is the single-team part. Each team could use it to cut its own work, order it, and judge what fits in a period. Whatever cross-team alignment method you choose would need a different source.
